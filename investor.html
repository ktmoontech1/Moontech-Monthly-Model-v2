Definitions and notes
ARR
Annualised revenue run rate: revenue in the last 3 months × 4. HeyMoon.ai is priced per campaign. Commission ARR is adjusted for seasonality using 2024–25 monthly weights.
ARR growth
Q3-26 run rate (Jul–Sep revenue × 4) vs Q2-26 run rate (Apr–Jun × 4). Calendar quarters, as for NRR.
Net revenue retention
Revenue in Q3-26 from brands that paid in Q2-26, ÷ their Q2-26 revenue. Calendar quarters.
Gross revenue retention
As NRR, excluding expansion: revenue kept from Q2-26 paying brands, capped at their Q2-26 level.
Spend per scaled brand
Total paid to date by the 8 brands that moved past Phase 1 (ROAS level 2+, excluding brands migrated from Commission), ÷ 8.
Retention after Phase 1
Annualised logo retention of brands that reached ROAS level 2 or above (one brand entered directly at a higher level and is included).
Logo churn
All HeyMoon.ai brands: 36% annualised, most of it at the end of Phase 1.
ARPA
ARR ÷ average active brands over the same 3 months.
LTV
ARPA × 50% gross margin ÷ annual logo churn.
CAC
All acquisition spend since November 2025 (incl. pre-launch) ÷ brands that have paid, excluding brands migrated from the Commission business.
CAC payback
CAC ÷ monthly gross profit per brand (ARPA × 50% ÷ 12).
Upgrade rate
Brands moving up one or more ROAS levels in the quarter ÷ brands active in it, including brands that started in the quarter.
Active brands
Brands with a live relationship per the ROAS ladder, including brands in their first campaign.
Changes since last update
ARPA now uses average active brands; CAC is fully loaded from November 2025; one pre-launch test brand is excluded from 2026 metrics.
Provisional
Total company ARR includes an estimate for one Commission client's Sep–Oct 2026 figures, pending final data. HeyMoon.ai figures are not affected.
